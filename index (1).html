Privacy Policy

Lasani Invoice · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Lasani Invoice (“the app”) is an invoicing, party ledger and payment tracking app for small businesses. It is developed and published by LV Traders, Shop U/105, Beroon Mughal Sarai, Raja Bazar, Rawalpindi, Pakistan (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information the app handles

  • Business data you enter: your business name, customers/parties (names, phone numbers), items, invoices, payments and notes. This data is stored on your device.
  • Cloud sync (optional): if you choose to create a workspace and sign in to Cloud sync, your business data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server so that your own devices can stay in sync. The email address you use to sign in is also stored. If you do not use Cloud sync, none of your business data leaves your device through the app.
  • Google Drive backup (optional): if you connect Google Drive, a backup file of your data is saved into your own Google Drive account. We do not have access to your Google Drive.
  • Subscription information: if you buy a subscription, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active.

2. Permissions

  • Contacts: used only when you tap the button to pick a customer from your phone contacts. The selected name and number are filled into the form. Your contact list is not uploaded or stored by us.
  • Internet / network state: used for optional Cloud sync, Google Drive backup, and checking subscription status.
  • Bluetooth / network printer: if you connect a receipt printer, invoice data is sent to that printer only.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to provide the features of the app: creating invoices, showing ledgers and reports, syncing between your devices, and backups you request. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal or business data with third parties.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only (a) with the services you choose to use (our sync server for Cloud sync, your own Google Drive for backup, Google Play for subscriptions), (b) when you yourself share an invoice, statement or report using WhatsApp, email or another app, or (c) if required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device stays until you delete it inside the app, clear the app data, or uninstall the app. If you use Cloud sync and want your cloud account and cloud data deleted, you can request this from inside the app or on our account deletion page, or by emailing lvtraders004@gmail.com. Backups in your own Google Drive can be deleted by you at any time.

7. Security

Cloud data is transmitted over HTTPS. You can protect the app on your device with a PIN or screen lock. Please keep your device secure and keep your backups in a safe place.

8. Children

The app is intended for business use by adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above.
