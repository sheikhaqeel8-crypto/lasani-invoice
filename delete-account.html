Delete Your Account and Data

App: Lasani Invoice · Developer: LV Traders

This page explains how to request deletion of your Lasani Invoice account and the data linked to it. It applies to users who created an account for the optional Cloud / Workspace sync. If you never created a Cloud account, nothing is stored on our server and you only need to delete the app data on your device (see below).

How to request deletion

  1. From inside the app: open Settings → Cloud / Workspace → Delete Account and send the request.
  2. Or by email: write to lvtraders004@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Lasani Invoice account. Please send it from the email address you use to sign in, and mention your business name, so we can verify the request.

What will be deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address).
  • Your Cloud workspace and all business data stored on our server: parties, items, invoices, payments and related records.

We delete this data within 30 days of verifying your request and confirm it to you by email. We do not keep a copy after deletion.

What is not removed by this request

  • Data on your own device: delete it inside the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Backups in your own Google Drive: these belong to you; delete them from your Google Drive.
  • Your Google Play subscription: deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it from Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
